<p align="center">
  <b>Guided cybersecurity learning and open Capture the Flag competitions.</b><br>
  An extension project of the Universidade Federal do Pampa (UNIPAMPA), built at <a href="https://ai-horizon-labs.github.io/">AI Horizon Labs</a>.

Capture the Flag (CTF) competitions are where most people get hands-on practice in security.
Each challenge hides a secret string, the flag, that can only be recovered by breaking,
decoding, or analyzing something. The challenges are written for people who already know the
ground, though, and a newcomer who opens one with no path leading up to it has little to hold
on to.

TocaiaCTF pairs each challenge with the concept it rests on and with support material in
Portuguese, English, and Spanish, arranged in tracks that go from the fundamentals to solving
on one's own. The project also runs open competitions, preceded by preparatory workshops, on
the Alegrete campus of UNIPAMPA with simultaneous remote participation, so anyone can take
part from wherever they are.

## What we are building

| | |
|---|---|
| Platform | A public web platform based on [CTFd](https://github.com/CTFd/CTFd), the open-source CTF server, extended with learning tracks, a link from each challenge to its support material, progress per participant and track, and challenges that run in isolated containers |
| Tracks | Web application security, applied cryptography, digital forensics, network traffic analysis, and reverse engineering, each graded from introductory to advanced |
| Languages | Portuguese, English, and Spanish |
| Competitions | An open edition every semester, on site and remote, with a certificate for every participant and a public session that walks through the solutions afterwards |
| Open access | The platform code, the challenges with commented solutions released after each edition, the support material, and a report of each edition |
| Audience | High school, technical, and undergraduate students, IT professionals, and anyone interested |

The platform is under development. Each part gets its own repository here as it is released.

## What this organization is for

TocaiaCTF holds the platform, the challenges, and the learning material of the project. They
will be released under open licenses, so that schools, federal institutes, and other
universities can run the material on their own, without depending on us.

*Tocaia* is Brazilian Portuguese, from the Tupi *to'kaya*, for an ambush: waiting, unseen, for
the right moment. The mark is the head of a Pampas cat (*gato-palheiro*), a small wild cat
named after the Pampa, the grasslands of southern Brazil, Uruguay, and Argentina. It is cut
out of a flag, the thing every challenge asks you to capture.
